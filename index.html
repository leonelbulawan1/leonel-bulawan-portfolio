      Privacy Notice – Leonel Bulawan

      Privacy Notice

      How this portfolio handles your information.

      Visitor statistics

      The site counts visits with GoatCounter and Vercel Web Analytics. They do not use cookies, and they only record general, anonymous details such as pages viewed, browser type and country.

      AI assistant

      Questions you type in the chat are sent to Google's Gemini service to generate an answer. This site does not save your conversations; your chat stays only in your browser tab and is cleared when you close it. Please don't share sensitive personal information in the chat.

      Messages you send

      When you use the contact form or "Leave a message", your name, email and message are delivered to my email through the FormSubmit service, and FormSubmit sends you an automatic confirmation email. I use your details only to reply to you.

      Voice chat

      If you talk to Leo with your voice, your browser turns your speech into text (in Chrome and Edge this is done by the browser maker's speech service), and only that text is sent to the AI, like a typed question. This site does not record or save your voice, and the microphone is used only while the mic button is on.

      Translation

      English and Filipino are translated by me. If you choose any other language in the Accessibility panel, the page text is sent to Google Translate to translate it, and Google's privacy policy applies.

      Your settings

      Your choices for theme, language, text size, contrast, motion and sound are saved only in your own browser. They are never sent to me.

      Other websites

      Project previews and links to social media, certificates and maps open other websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

      Questions or removal

      To ask about a message you sent or to have it deleted, just send me an email.
